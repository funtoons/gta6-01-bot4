**Solmarina** is an original open-world crime game for the
[make.fun GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/c/gta6-01): a sun-soaked coastal
city you explore on foot and by car, with traffic, pedestrians, a wanted
level and police who respond, six story missions, combat, taxi fares,
vigilante work, races, hidden packages, and a save system.

You play Rio Calloway, an ex-wheelman back in Solmarina owing $50,000 to
loan shark Big Alonzo. Fixer Mama Zola gives him jobs; rival crew The
Gulls, led by Vic Marlowe, wants him gone.

## How to play

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser. No build step, no network:
every file the game loads lives under `game/`.

- **WASD / arrows** — walk and drive
- **Mouse** — aim, **click** — shoot / punch
- **E** — enter or exit a car, start jobs
- **Space** — handbrake, **Shift** — sprint
- **R** — radio station, **M** — world map, **P / Esc** — pause

## What is in the game

- A seeded city of six districts: Neon Mile, Porto Verde, Palm Court,
  The Yards, Sunnyside and Bayside beach, with a road graph, traffic
  lights, palms, billboards, a marina, a stadium and a fountain plaza.
- Wanted 0–5 stars: foot cops, patrol cars, roadblocks and a police
  helicopter with a spotlight at four stars; break line of sight to
  evade, or pay for a respray at Sunshine Spray.
- Six story missions, plus taxi fares, vigilante chases in a police car,
  three checkpoint races, stunt jumps and 20 hidden packages.
- A HUD with a live minimap, a police dispatch panel, weapon slots,
  speed, cash, clock, respect rank and subtitled dialogue.
- Synthesized WebAudio sound: engine hum, sirens, gunshots and three
  procedural radio stations. No audio files.

An original game. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games.
